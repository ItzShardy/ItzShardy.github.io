Asked, answered

The stuff people actually ask before DMing.

Two months. Everything here says exactly how finished it is — I'd rather show a real TODO list than a fake portfolio.

Can I pay you for work?

Small jobs, yes. We agree scope and price in writing first, I fix my own bugs free within 7 days. Full terms on the commissions page.

Will you join my server team?

Probably not — I'm heads-down learning. Feedback swaps and small one-off jobs, always.

What versions do you build for?

Paper 1.21 on Java 17. If your server runs something ancient, I'll tell you straight up if I can't help.

Do you share my server files around?

No. Your files stay yours, DMs stay private, and there's no mailing list to end up on.
