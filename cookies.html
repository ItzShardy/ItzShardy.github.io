Cookies

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. There is nothing to accept or reject for this site itself.

What is stored in your browser

  • Cookie notice flag: when you press “Got it”, I save shardy-cookie-choice=acknowledged in your browser's localStorage so the bar doesn't reappear. It never leaves your device. Clear site data to remove it.
  • Nothing else. No login, no cart, no preferences stored.

Third party that acts like cookies

No webfonts: the site uses system fonts, so no font provider sees your visit. The hero card fetches my public Discord presence (api.lanyard.rest) and avatar (cdn.discordapp.com) — those see technical data. No YouTube embeds, no analytics, no ad networks.

Do I need consent?

Because I set no tracking cookies, no consent is legally required for my own code in most regions. I still show a small notice so you know what's happening. If you embed my page or add analytics later, re-check your local cookie-consent rules (EU ePrivacy/GDPR, UK PECR, etc.).
