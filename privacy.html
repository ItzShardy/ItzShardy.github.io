Hobby Minecraft developer: Shardy (ItzShardy). Contact: Discord im_shardy · email newspiderman72@gmail.com · GitHub. I am an individual, not a registered company. No address is listed because there is no studio or shop to visit.

What I collect: basically nothing

  • No accounts, no forms, no newsletters. This site has no contact form, so nothing you type here is stored on this site.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies itself. The notice at the bottom only remembers your “Got it” in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent to me. Details on the Cookies page.
