Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Plain English, not legal advice. If you run commissions in the EU/UK, check your local GDPR rules.

Who I am

Hobby Minecraft developer: Shardy (MostCallMeShardy / ItzShardy). Contact: Discord im_shardy · email newspiderman72@gmail.com · GitHub. I am an individual, not a registered company. No address is listed because there is no studio or shop to visit.

What I collect: basically nothing

  • No accounts, no forms that store, no newsletters. The contact form sends via your own email app or Formspree if I enable it — nothing is kept on this site itself.
  • If you email or DM me, I see your handle/address and message so I can reply. I don't sell it, share it, or add you to any list. I delete threads I no longer need.
  • No analytics, no pixels, no tracking scripts. I don't run Google Analytics or any ad tracker.

Third parties that may see technical data

  • GitHub Pages (hosting): serves these files. GitHub may keep standard server logs (e.g. IP, date) to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy docs.
  • Fonts: system fonts only — no font downloads, no extra requests.
  • Discord presence (Lanyard + Discord CDN): the hero card fetches my public presence from api.lanyard.rest and my avatar from cdn.discordapp.com. Those services may see visitors' IPs. No YouTube embeds, no maps.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies itself. The notice only remembers your “Got it” in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent to me. Details on the Cookies page.
