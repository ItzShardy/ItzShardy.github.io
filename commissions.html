Commissions & refunds

Last updated: October 2026 · I sell nothing on this site. Work only starts after we agree in writing.

No shop, nothing to refund today

There is no checkout, no instant download and no paid product on this site. So there is nothing to charge back. If that ever changes, I will add the seller name, prices with taxes, and delivery terms here first.

If you ask me for paid help

  • Step 1 — free chat: DM Discord im_shardy or email newspiderman72@gmail.com with your server version (e.g. Paper 1.21), what you want, and your deadline. I reply if I can do it.
  • Step 2 — written agreement: scope, price, timeline and revision count, confirmed by both sides in email/Discord. No agreement = no work = no payment.
  • Step 3 — delivery: files or setup help plus short setup notes. You test within 7 days and report bugs with logs.

Refund rules I follow

  • Full refund if I haven't started your commission when you cancel.
  • Partial refund if I've started but not delivered: you pay only for time already agreed in writing.
  • Fix-first after delivery: I fix bugs I introduced, free, if you report within 7 days with steps and logs. Refund instead if I can't fix within a reasonable second try.
  • No refund for: change of mind after delivery, bans/issues caused by your host or other plugins, or work you edited yourself before asking me to fix it.

Payments

Only through a method we both agree in writing (never gift cards, never “friends and family” pressure). I never ask for your passwords. Keep receipts — I keep mine too.

Minors

If you're under 18, a parent/guardian must approve and pay. I don't take on large or time-critical server work while I'm still learning — I'll tell you honestly if your job is too big for me.

Questions first? Email me — no commitment.
